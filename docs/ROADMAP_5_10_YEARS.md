# 5–10 Year Roadmap — Practical Net-Positive Fusion (PATSAGi locked)

**Horizon:** 2026–2036  
**Goal:** Contribute open, high-signal advances that accelerate commercial net-positive electricity and materials readiness.

## Phase 1 (2026–2028) — Foundations & Materials
- Comprehensive open literature + experimental map of first-wall, divertor, and blanket materials under high neutron flux.
- High-fidelity simulation scaffolds for thermal cycling + radiation damage (leveraging existing Ra-Thor GPU compute).
- Tritium breeding ratio models with realistic lithium/lead-lithium blankets.
- Synergy studies with ambient/high-Tc magnets from companion lattice.

## Phase 2 (2028–2031) — Integration & Validation
- Closed-loop breeding designs targeting TBR > 1.05 with manufacturable components.
- Grid integration & load-following strategies for early commercial plants.
- Safety / non-proliferation valence scoring under TOLC 8.
- Open datasets and reproducible simulation results published here.

## Phase 3 (2031–2036) — Abundance Multiplication
- Validated materials & systems contributions fed into Daedalus-Skin and interstellar layers.
- Support for multiple private/public pathways (Helion-style pulsed, CFS tokamak, stellarators, He3).
- Continuous PATSAGi review ensuring zero-harm and maximum thriving impact.

All phases remain under permanent PATSAGi + TOLC 8 governance.
