# Phase 1 Concrete Research Questions (2026–2028)

PATSAGi-locked priority questions for foundations & materials:

1. **First-wall / divertor survival**: Which candidate materials (W, advanced alloys, composites, liquid metals) maintain structural integrity and acceptable erosion after 10–50 dpa + realistic thermal cycling? Publish open comparative datasets.
2. **Breeding blanket performance**: Achievable TBR under realistic geometry, neutron spectrum, and lithium inventory constraints. Target >1.05 with manufacturable designs.
3. **Low-activation requirement**: Activation products and waste classification for leading candidates after end-of-life.
4. **HTS magnet synergy**: How much size/cost reduction is available for compact devices when ambient or higher-Tc magnets become practical (cross-link High-Tc lattice).
5. **Off-normal event resilience**: Transient heat loads and disruption handling for net-positive duty cycle.

All answers must be grounded in experiment or high-fidelity validated simulation.
