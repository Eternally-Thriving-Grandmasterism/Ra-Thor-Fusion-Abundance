# TOLC 8 Ethics & Zero-Harm Note

This discovery lattice operates under the non-bypassable TOLC 8 Living Mercy Gates.

- **Truth**: No exaggerated timelines or unvalidated claims.
- **Order / Safety**: Materials and systems work must consider activation, waste, and operational safety.
- **Non-proliferation**: Designs and data that could meaningfully lower barriers to weapons-relevant knowledge are restricted or redacted under council review.
- **Abundance & Service**: Primary goal is clean, abundant energy for all sentience.

Any contribution that violates the zero-harm floor will be rejected by PATSAGi deliberation.
