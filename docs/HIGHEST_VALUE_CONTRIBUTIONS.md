## Primary (Highest Value)
1. **Validated TBR numbers** — experimental or high-fidelity neutron-transport results (OpenMC / MCNP / Serpent) with clear geometry, nuclear data, and uncertainty.
2. **Real materials performance data** — first-wall / divertor candidates under relevant dpa + thermal cycling, with primary references.
3. **Any measured or rigorously simulated net-power or confinement results** tied to published devices.

## Secondary
- Improvements that replace illustrative scaffolds with validated models.
- Grid-integration or cost analyses grounded in published projections.

## Lower Priority
Further pure documentation or additional illustrative scaffolds without new experimental or validated numerical content.
